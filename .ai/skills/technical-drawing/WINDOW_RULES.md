# Window Technical Drawing Rules

## Approved semantic and visual conventions

- A window may contain one or multiple fields.
- Fields may independently be FIXED or OPERABLE. Field type is separate from a functional region role.
- Dividers partition window structure; do not infer their physical catalogue facts from a visual reference.
- An operable sash is drawn inside its corresponding field.
- An opening symbol belongs to the actual sash, not to the outer frame or an unrelated field.
- Opening mode and handing are explicit semantics. Do not infer them from field position or a generic window label.
- Technical dimension chains normally sit outside the technical sketch and originate at actual represented edges.
- Keep internal labels and width × height pills out of the clean production-style sketch; semantic identity belongs in the inspector/cards.

## Human-approved reference descriptors

- Two adjacent fields may show one fixed/non-opening region and one tilt-turn/two-axis operable sash.
- A three-part example may have a center tilt-turn sash and fixed side regions.
- A three-region example may combine a side-hung sash, a fixed region, and a tilt-turn sash; symbols differ by explicitly represented opening mode.
- A four-part example may show two central tilt-turn sashes and fixed outer regions, with mirrored symbols where explicitly shown.
- Example dimensions and equal-looking proportions are EXAMPLE ONLY, never defaults or rules.

## Limits

These rules do not define profile/catalogue-specific geometry, sash overlap, production dimensions, or inferred handing. See [opening symbol rules](OPENING_SYMBOL_RULES.md) and [evidence rules](REFERENCE_EVIDENCE_RULES.md).
