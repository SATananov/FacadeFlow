# Relation Token Rules

Tokens such as `L_Fr`, `R_Fr`, `U_Fr`, and `D_Fr` describe source relation contexts. They are not geometry.

`SglobkaDelitel` identifies imported divider-joint operation evidence. For KMG contexts, known imported rows include:

- `BeamHorizontalKMG4k`: `L_Fr;19;SglobkaDelitel;POS[];0;MM1` and `R_Fr;19;SglobkaDelitel;POS[];0;MM4`.
- `BeamVerticalKMG4k`: `U_Fr;19;SglobkaDelitel;POS[];0;MM1` and `D_Fr;19;SglobkaDelitel;POS[];0;MM1`.

These rows prove the existence of a divider-joint operation record only. They do not prove notch, overlap, rebate, cutter, or machine geometry.
