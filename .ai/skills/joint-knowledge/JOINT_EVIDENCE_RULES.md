# Joint Evidence Rules

`DATABASE_RULE_EVIDENCE` means that an operation/token was copied from an imported source row. It does not validate the physical result.

Promote a relationship to `CATALOGUE_VERIFIED` only after an independent authoritative catalogue, section, or technical document explicitly supports it.

For every token preserve system, operation id/name, relation token, operation code/name, position expression, parameters, machine/macro marker, and source provenance.

The following remain UNKNOWN unless separately proven:

- notch contour;
- overlap, rebate, or inset;
- cutter/tool path;
- final cut angle or cut length;
- machine-ready coordinates.
